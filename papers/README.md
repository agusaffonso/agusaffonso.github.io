Put paper PDFs in this folder.

Name them in lowercase with hyphens and no accents, e.g. `information-frictions.pdf`.

Then link a paper's title in `index.html` by replacing its `<span class="t">` with:

    <a class="t" href="papers/information-frictions.pdf">Title of the Paper</a>

The small arrow after the title appears automatically on linked titles.
